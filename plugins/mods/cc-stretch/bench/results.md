## Coverage: does the skim still carry the answer?

For 20 of the replayed replies, a blind reader saw only one version and noted every decision, action and result it was told about. A grader listed the original's must-know points (at most eight) and checked both readers' notes against the same list, with the two readers under neutral labels in alternating order. The original is the control: it shows how strict the grader is.

| reader saw | must-know points | caught | partly | missed | coverage, all points | coverage, top three |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| skim | 158 | 39 | 63 | 56 | **45%** | **68%** |
| original | 158 | 156 | 1 | 1 | **99%** | **99%** |

Coverage counts a caught point as 1 and a partly caught one as 0.5. The grader lists points most important first; "top three" scores only those.
